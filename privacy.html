Privacy Policy

Publisher: M2R-H. Last updated: October 2026.

Summary

Krisalya PDF processes your documents only on your device. The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data, any document or any usage statistics. It works without an account and without an internet connection.

Data processed

  • Your documents (PDF, images, office files you open or create) are read and written on your device, in the app workspace and at the locations you choose. They never leave the device.
  • Change log: for each document the app locally records the date, the operation, the device session account name and, if you entered it, the name you chose in “About”. The log stays in the app workspace on your device and is only exported when you ask for it (CSV or PDF).
  • Text recognition (OCR) runs on an engine built into the app, offline. On macOS and iOS you may choose the system recognition engine (Apple Vision), which also runs on the device.
  • Digital signatures: certificate verification uses the system trust store and the certificates you add; no network revocation request is made.

Data not collected

The app does not collect identifiers, email address, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, usage data, crash reports or document content. No third‑party service (analytics, advertising, tracking) is integrated.

Permissions

  • Files: only the files you select through the system picker or open with the app.
  • No network, camera, microphone, location or contacts permission.

Retention and deletion

Working copies, versions and logs are kept in the app workspace until you delete the document (trash emptied automatically after thirty days) or uninstall the app. External files are never deleted by the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data whatever the user's age.

Contact

M2R-H, through the contact address shown on the app's store listing.
